# CS 370: Pirate Intelligent Agent

## Project Overview

In this project, I created a pirate intelligent agent able to find treasure at the end of a maze utilizing a deep Q learning model. Through this project, I demonstrated that combining reinforcement learning and neural networks enables the solving of pathfinding problems.

My task was to create the Q training algorithm and establish the training protocol to allow the pirate to utilize past experiences through the use of the provided starter code. Starter code contained the maze environment, an experience replay feature, and the overall structure of the Jupyter Notebook.

When working on my solution, I incorporated an exploration strategy, utilized a neural network to train the model, and made adjustments to the target network to enhance the agent's abilities. After completing the model, I found that it had a 100 percent training win rate after 183 epochs and was able to reach the treasure from all qualified start locations.

## What do computer scientists do and why does it matter?

Computer scientists employ programming techniques and computational methods to resolve issues. They plan and write software, analyze data, optimize current systems, and develop new technology that enables processes to operate more efficiently.

This is relevant since computer science improves many industries including health care, financial services, transportation, etc. During this course, I learned how machine learning may be employed to provide answers to problems where an automated system will need to make decisions based upon previous experience instead of through a predefined series of steps.

## How do I approach a problem as a computer scientist?

To approach a problem as a computer scientist, I initially identify what I am trying to achieve and comprehend what requirements exist. Then I break down the issue into manageable parts and decide on which tools or algorithms would most likely assist in resolving each component.

While working on this project, I needed to understand how the maze environment operated, how reward feedback would influence the pirate's actions, and how the neural network would be able to learn from prior experiences. Testing played a significant role during this project as well. It enabled me to assess how effectively the pirate performed and determine where improvements were needed.

Overall, this experience further emphasized the significance of building solutions incrementally and measuring the performance of those solutions.

## What are my ethical responsibilities toward the end user and organization?

As a computer scientist, one has a duty to create systems that are reliable, safe from unauthorized access, and designed with the needs of users in mind. This consists of ensuring that personally identifiable information is protected, testing software to identify and correct potential errors, and providing transparency regarding the limitations of a given system.

Additionally, artificial intelligence adds other risks due to potential bias in decision making generated by AI models. Organizations rely on developers to adhere to established industry norms and minimize risk in their coding practices.

Through this course, I have come to realize that generating an effective AI system is dependent upon more than just having a successful outcome from training. In addition, it is essential to assess if the system operates as intended and whether its decisions can be relied upon.
